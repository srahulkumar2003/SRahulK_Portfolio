# Implementation Notes

## Design pattern used

The portfolio follows a dark cinematic engineering pattern:

- Dark background with animated grid.
- Blue/cyan neon accents.
- Glassmorphism cards.
- Floating skill orbit around the profile photo.
- Smooth Framer Motion scroll animations.
- GSAP background orb animation.
- Recruiter-friendly section order.

## Section order

1. Hero
2. About
3. Skills
4. Experience
5. Projects
6. Research
7. Certifications
8. Resume and Education
9. Contact

## Why this pattern fits Rahul's profile

This layout is optimized for Backend, DevOps, Cloud and Full Stack roles. It gives more importance to technical projects, certificates and research instead of unnecessary decoration.
